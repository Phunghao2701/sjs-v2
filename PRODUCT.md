# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- Researchers tracking publications, journals, authors, topics, and collaborations.
- Administrators managing platform accounts, journal data, and analysis sources.

## Product Purpose

Trending System helps researchers explore scholarly output and monitor research projects, while giving administrators a surface to maintain the data and access that support those workflows.

## Brand Personality

Clear, credible, focused.

## Anti-references

- Inconsistent page layouts, control styles, or navigation between route families.
- Decorative motion or color that competes with research data.
- Empty visualizations that hide labels or make metrics hard to interpret.

## Design Principles

- Make dense research information easy to scan and compare.
- Keep a shared navigation, spacing, and component vocabulary across researcher and admin pages.
- Use the brand accent for primary actions and selection states; preserve distinct colors for data semantics.
- Make loading, empty, error, focus, and disabled states explicit.
- Keep routes, permissions, and data behavior stable while the interface is unified.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls.
- Preserve visible keyboard focus and meaningful labels.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Keep core page structures usable on narrow screens and at zoomed text sizes.
